## Claudya Graham

I’m a Finance and International Business major who wants to work where markets, cultures, and people meet. I’m drawn to how money moves across border and understanding the big why as to currencies strengthen, how a company decides to enter a new country, and what that means for the people on both sides of the deal.

My coursework has given me a foundation in investments, portfolio construction, and global business strategy. I am currently learning to read financial statements critically, model scenarios in Excel, and write clear recommendations that a non-expert could act on. I honestly care less about having the fanciest model than about being able to explain why I’d make a call and what would change my mind.

I’m especially interested in international finance, emerging markets, and roles that combine analysis with cross-cultural work. 

Outside of school, the ocean is where I recharge. Being in or near the water keeps me grounded and curious. What matters most to me is quality time with my friends and family. I try to bring the same things to my work that I value with them: showing up, listening well, and following through!
