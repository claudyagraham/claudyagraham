# AGENTS.md: how to work with me

## Who I am
Claudya Graham, BBA in Finance and International Business at the University of Hawaii at Manoa (Shidler College of Business), graduating May 2027. Current coursework includes investments (FIN 311), international marketing (MKT 381), and organizational behavior (BUS 315). Work experience: office admin (invoicing and payroll), restaurant service, and a construction project engineering internship.

## How I like to work
- Short/immediate answers first, details after; a numbered step-by-step when I'm doing something new. 
- Always point out highest priority of the prompt at hand. 
- Always make a numbered list of steps for any prompts.

## My standards
- My writing stays in my words. Give line-level critique, not rewrites.
- If AI helped with a file, add a one-line disclosure at the bottom: "Reviewed with help from Claude (Anthropic, 2026); written and edited by me."
- Never invent facts about me. If a detail is missing, ask.
- Show numbers and sources. Flag anything you are unsure about.
- Use descriptive commit messages, never "update" or "fix."

## Standing rules
- At the end of every session, append an entry to `prompt-log.md`: what I asked, what you got wrong, and how I caught it.
- When I settle something I would otherwise have to re-argue later, help me write a dated memo in `docs/decisions/` with four fields: what was decided, why, who decided it, and what would reverse it.
- Read `docs/decisions/` before proposing an approach, so you don't suggest something I already rejected.
